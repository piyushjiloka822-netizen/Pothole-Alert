About the Portal

MCL Pothole Grievance Reporting & Tracking System — Empowering Citizens for Better Roads

🏛️

Our Mission

The Municipal Corporation of Lucknow (MCL) Pothole Grievance Portal is an e-Governance initiative under the Government of Uttar Pradesh to empower citizens with a transparent, accountable, and technology-driven platform for reporting and tracking road infrastructure issues.

🎯

Vision

A Lucknow with smooth, safe, and well-maintained roads for every citizen — powered by technology and civic participation.

📊

Transparency

Every complaint is tracked with a unique Ticket ID. Real-time status updates ensure complete transparency from reporting to resolution.

⚡

Speed

GPS-enabled location pinning, photo evidence, and automated severity classification enable faster response and repair scheduling.

🤝

Accountability

Auto-escalation after 30 days of inaction ensures no complaint goes unresolved. Senior officers are notified automatically.

🌐

Bilingual Access

Full Hindi and English language support ensures every citizen of Lucknow can access and use this portal comfortably.

♿

GIGW Compliant

Fully compliant with Government of India Guidelines for Indian Government Websites (GIGW) — accessibility, standards, and security.

RTI Act 2005

🏛️ Right to Information Act, 2005

Every citizen of India has the right to seek information from public authorities. Under Section 6 of the RTI Act, any citizen can request information held by or under the control of any public authority. MCL Roads Department is committed to providing transparent and timely responses to all RTI applications.

📝 How to File an RTI Application

1

Prepare Application

Write your RTI application in English or Hindi on plain paper. Mention clearly: (i) Subject, (ii) Information sought, (iii) Period of information.

2

Pay Application Fee

Attach ₹10 as application fee via IPO / Demand Draft / Court Fee Stamp payable to "Municipal Commissioner, MCL, Lucknow".

3

Submit Application

Submit by post or in person at MCL office, or online via the central RTI portal: rtionline.gov.in

4

Receive Response

PIO will respond within 30 days of receipt. If unsatisfied, file first appeal within 30 days.

❓ RTI — Frequently Asked Questions

What information can I seek under RTI?
You can seek any information that is held by MCL Roads Department — road construction budgets, repair expenditures, contractor details, timeline records, audit reports, etc.
Is BPL category exempt from fees?
Yes. Citizens below the poverty line (BPL) are exempt from RTI application fees. Attach a BPL certificate with your application.
Can I file RTI online?
Yes. Use the Government of India's RTI Online Portal at rtionline.gov.in for online submissions and tracking.
